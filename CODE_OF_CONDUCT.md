# Code of Conduct

BKKT is built for operators, data engineers, and platform teams who often work
under production pressure. We want the project space to feel direct, kind, and
useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Assume good intent, but make risks and tradeoffs explicit.
- Prefer constructive technical feedback over personal criticism.
- Help newcomers understand the operational context behind decisions.
- Keep incident discussions blameless and focused on recovery and prevention.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Publicly exposing private infrastructure, secrets, customer data, or
  incident-sensitive details.
- Dismissing safety concerns around production operations.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions after maintainers ask to refocus.

## Reporting

If you see behavior that makes participation unsafe or unproductive, report it
through the project's security/contact channel described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will review reports privately and take
appropriate action.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, restrict participation, or take
other action when behavior harms the project community or operational safety.
